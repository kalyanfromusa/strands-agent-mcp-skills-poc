# Product
ShopAssist is a low-cost PoC: a **generic, skills-driven Strands agent** for a fictional online store's customer support
(order status, returns/refunds, support tickets). The agent has no business logic. Skills (`skills/*/SKILL.md`) hold the
procedures, and MCP tools behind Amazon Bedrock AgentCore Gateway hold data and rule enforcement.
Audience: architects and engineers evaluating AgentCore Runtime, Gateway and Identity, and Terraform vs boto3 delivery.
